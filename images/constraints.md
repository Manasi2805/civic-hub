
The Five Hard Constraints
#             Constraint                      Status      
1            Fake, spam and harassment reports   ⚠️      
2            Unclear jurisdiction                ✅      
3            Prioritisation beyond "most votes"  ✅      
4             Bad input                          ✅      
5            Works without internet              ⚠️      
1. Fake, Spam and Harassment ReportsApproach: Uses a multi-signal verification engine (verification_engine.py) assessing GPS coordinate precision, image file metadata presence, text length, and regex profanity matching. Scores below 50% are routed into a "Needs Verification" review queue for municipal staff evaluation rather than immediate crew dispatch.Anonymity trade-off: Citizen reporting allows pseudonymity without mandatory Aadhaar/KYC identity checks, but requires a valid contact session (civic_citizen_user in client storage) to rate-limit submissions per device and suppress automated flooding.Code: backend/app/services/verification_engine.py, frontend/src/pages/CitizenLogin.jsx
 2. Unclear JurisdictionApproach: Evaluates GPS coordinates against Mysuru municipal boundary GeoJSON polygons using point-in-polygon tests. A 50-meter buffer boundary detects edge-cases between adjacent wards.What happens in a boundary case: If a pin falls inside an overlapping buffer or outside all known ward boundaries, jurisdiction is assigned as Mysuru (Unassigned) with low routing confidence, moving the ticket into a shared triaging queue where zonal supervisors reassign authority manually.Code: backend/app/services/routing_engine.py, backend/data/mysuru_boundaries.geojson
3. Prioritisation  Formula / rules:$$\text{Priority Score} = (\text{Base Severity} \times 0.4) + (\text{Location Weight} \times 0.3) + (\text{Cluster Count} \times 0.2) + (\text{Age in Hours} \times 0.1)$$Base Severity: Potholes / open drains (High = 3), garbage piles (Medium = 2), non-functional streetlights (Low = 1).Location Weight: Hospital zones, schools, and major transit routes carry a $1.5\times$ multiplier.Why not simply "most votes": Pure voting creates an echo chamber where densely populated commercial centers dominate public attention, leaving critical hazards in quieter, lower-income, or peripheral wards completely neglected.Code: backend/app/services/insight_engine.py, backend/app/routes/complaints.py
